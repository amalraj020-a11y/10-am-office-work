## Target business

CAFE LEO, 14 Q, Dum Dum Road, RBI Staff Quarters, Bir Para, Paikpara, Kolkata, West Bengal 700030. Public listings show phone `+91 70034 49639`, Facebook `cafeleo10`, cuisine categories including fast food and beverages, menu categories including salads, starters, pasta, burgers and sandwiches, and opening hours around 12:00 PM–10:00 PM.

The target was selected because the public listings reviewed showed a Facebook presence and directory listings, but no dedicated official domain for this Kolkata business. Confirm with the owner before publishing any final facts.

## Competitor reference

Orange Café & Dîner in Bir, Himachal Pradesh was used as a structural reference for a welcoming café presentation, local atmosphere, food-led sections, and clear visit details. The CAFE LEO demo uses original copy, colors, layout, and illustration styling rather than copying the competitor site.
