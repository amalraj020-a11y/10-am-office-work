# Amala workflow

## 10:00–11:00 AM IST

1. Research local shops and small businesses.
2. Verify identity, location, services, hours, social profiles, reviews, and official website status.
3. Keep only qualified businesses with no functioning official website.
4. Build one small, working demo with free tools and test mobile and desktop views.
5. Save the business details, evidence, demo link, code path, screenshot, proposed price, and next action in the tracker.
6. Prepare respectful outreach and wait for owner response.

## Approval gates

- No paid tools, domains, ads, or subscriptions without user approval.
- No production deployment or ownership transfer before acceptance and payment confirmation.
- The user handles or approves payment collection.
- Do not spam, misrepresent the business, or claim ownership of the business.

## Suggested free stack

- GitHub for source control.
- Vercel free tier for preview deployments when connected.
- HTML/CSS/JavaScript or another lightweight framework.
- Free public research sources, with URLs recorded as evidence.
